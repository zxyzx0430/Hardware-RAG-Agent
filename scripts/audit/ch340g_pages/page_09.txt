5. Function Description
CH340 chip has built-in USB pull-up resistor, UD+ and UD- pins must be connected to USB bus directly.  
CH340 chip has built-in power-on reset circuit. CH340B also provides low active external reset pin. 
CH340G/CH340T/CH340R chips need to work with 12MHz clock signal supplied to XI pin. Generally, 
clock signal is generated crystal oscillation with inverter in CH340. The peripheral circuit needs to place a crystal 
of 12MHz between XI and XO, and connect to a capacitor to ground separately. 
CH340C, CH340E and CH340B chip have built-in clock generator, no external crystal and oscillating 
capacitor required.
